New / Correction Request

Capture the supplier request fields that Visual Builder sends to ORDS. HTML checks are immediate; ATP checks are authoritative.
Correction required: Reviewer asked for bank country confirmation and tax certificate attachment. Edit the same request version, then submit again.

Corporate Identity

Address, Site & Contact

Country, address line 1, and city are blocking address fields. State and postal code feed the configurable address-quality score when the country expects them.

Financial Routing

Bank details can be absent at initial request. If present, ATP encrypts the value, stores a masked display value, and uses a protected fingerprint for exact duplicate matching.

Supporting Document

Missing expected documents become validation or risk findings in REQUEST_ASSESSMENT. The prototype stores supplier document metadata and BLOB content in REQUEST_DOCUMENT.

Justification Risk Not Applied

Reviewer choice: no added risk. Reason: the reviewer did not confirm Gemini's concern. Final risk remains the deterministic score: 75.

+3 Adjustment

Reviewer choice: add 3 points for minor justification risk. Final score preview: 78. The decision is stored on REQUEST_ASSESSMENT and audited in ACTION_HISTORY.

+5 Adjustment

Reviewer choice: add 5 points for moderate justification risk. Final score preview: 80. The decision is stored on REQUEST_ASSESSMENT and audited in ACTION_HISTORY.

+10 Adjustment

Reviewer choice: add 10 points for serious justification risk. Final score preview: 85. The decision is stored on REQUEST_ASSESSMENT and audited in ACTION_HISTORY.

Decision Outcome Preview

Choose a reviewer decision above to preview the business outcome and status transition.

Accept Outcome

Decision: accept the request. Reason: tax and bank details are verified and warnings are acknowledged. Status moves from UNDER_REVIEW to APPROVED, then ATP creates a Fusion creation job.

Reject Outcome

Decision: reject the request. Reason: duplicate risk and business justification were not resolved. Status moves from UNDER_REVIEW to REJECTED. No Fusion job is created.

Mark Duplicate Outcome

Decision: mark as duplicate. Existing supplier: SUP-09716. Reason: exact tax ID match. Status moves from UNDER_REVIEW to DUPLICATE, and the existing supplier reference is stored in action history.

Correction Outcome

Decision: send correction to the requester. Reason: confirm bank country and attach tax certificate. Status moves from UNDER_REVIEW to CORRECTION_REQUIRED, and the requester sees an Edit action.
